What apartments actually cost in Georgia

Average $1,395–1,500 per square metre in Tbilisi and Batumi, plus the costs nobody quotes: finishing, furniture, service charges, valuation and legal fees.

The headline numbers

At the end of 2025, Colliers Georgia put the average residential price per square metre at about $1,500 in both Tbilisi and Batumi. TBC Capital's figure for Batumi specifically is around $1,395 per m². Agents in Batumi report annual growth of 8–10%, and Colliers expected no price decrease through 2026.

Averages hide an enormous range. In the same city and the same month you will find $700 per square metre in an outer district and $3,000 per square metre for a finished first-row sea-view apartment in a branded tower. The average is useful for one thing only: telling you whether a price you have been quoted is roughly in the market or nowhere near it.

The costs that are never in the price list

A great many buyers budget the purchase price and are then surprised by the total. Plan for all of this:
  • Finishing, if the apartment is sold as white or green frame — roughly $250–450 per m² depending on standard. On a 60 m² apartment that is $15,000–27,000, and it is not optional if you intend to let.
  • Furniture and appliances — a short-let apartment needs a full fit-out, not a sofa.
  • Service charges — a branded tower with a pool, spa, reception and security costs meaningfully more per month than an old block, every month, whether or not the apartment is occupied.
  • Certified valuation — $100–300, essential if residency is your reason for buying.
  • Your own lawyer — a few hundred dollars, the best value in the entire transaction.
  • Registration fee at the Public Registry — small, but budget it.

What moves the price per metre

Five things, roughly in order of impact:
  • Distance from the sea in Batumi — first row against second row is the single largest step in the city.
  • Finished or frame. Comparing a finished price to a white-frame price is the most common way people convince themselves one building is cheap.
  • Floor and view.
  • Whether balcony and terrace area is counted in the quoted square metres. This is where the per-metre price quietly shifts by 10–15% between two developers who look like they are quoting the same thing.
  • Handover date. A 2029 delivery should be materially cheaper than a finished building, because you are carrying the risk and losing the income until then.
When you compare two buildings, normalise for all five before you conclude anything. Most 'bargains' in this market are one of these five differences wearing a discount.

Common questions

How much is a residency-qualifying apartment?

It must appraise at $150,000 or more. At around $1,500 per square metre that is roughly a 100 m² apartment, or a smaller one in a premium building. Buying at exactly $150,000 leaves no margin if the valuation comes in low.

Are prices still rising in 2026?

Batumi agents report 8–10% annual growth and Colliers expected no decrease through 2026. New supply is arriving, which they judged is offset by banks financing fewer new projects. Treat it as a forecast, not a promise.

What does it cost to finish a white-frame apartment?

Roughly $250–450 per square metre depending on the standard you want. Get a written scope and a fixed price from a contractor before you commit to a frame purchase.

Figures checked September 2026 against Colliers Georgia, Galt & Taggart, TBC Capital and Georgian government sources. Rules change; confirm anything you are about to act on.
