The one that finishes first

Every unit here starts above $150,000 and the building hands over in December 2026. That combination matters: the residency permit needs registered ownership, not a signed contract, so a project completing in 2026 puts you in the queue two to three years ahead of one completing in 2028 or 2029.

What you get: a rooftop infinity pool (25 m, heated to 28°C), private beach access, two restaurants including a panoramic one, a fitness centre, a children's playground, a conference hall and secured parking. 118 one-bedrooms and two duplexes among the 456.

Where this project sits against the residency line

Studios open at $155,000 and the one-bedroom Lux at $261,000, so on the developer's own price list the one-bedrooms are the residency units and the studios sit only just above the line — close enough that a certified valuation could bring one under it.

Ask for the appraisal before you sign, not after. And note the thing that actually makes this building useful for a permit: it hands over in December 2026. Registered ownership is the requirement, and this is the only project on the list that reaches it next year.
