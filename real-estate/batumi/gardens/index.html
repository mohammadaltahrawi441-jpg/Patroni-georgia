Still early enough to choose

At 35% sold you can still pick the floor, the aspect and the unit type — which on a sea-facing building is most of the value. Ceilings are 3.3 metres. A pool with sea views, a sauna, a co-working space, a fitness centre, restaurants and bars, gardens, 24/7 reception and parking.

Two dates, not one: handover December 2027, full turnkey completion June 2028. Plan your rental income from the second date, not the first. Instalments run to 36 months at 0%, from about $1,535 a month on a 15% ($9,700) first payment.

The roof is the amenity

The pool deck runs the length of the roof with the sea on one side and the ridge on the other, heated so it works outside July and August — which matters, because the difference between a summer-only building and a year-round one is most of your rental income.

Add the sauna, the co-working space, the fitness centre and the gardens at ground level, and what you are buying at Kvariati is a building people can live in through a Batumi winter, not just visit in a Batumi August.

Fifty metres of beach in front and a heated pool above it. That combination is what you are paying the premium for.
