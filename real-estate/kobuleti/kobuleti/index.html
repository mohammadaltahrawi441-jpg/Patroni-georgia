Five years is a long time

The monthly payment is the lowest on this list — about $1,213 — and the first payment is only 10%, roughly $7,500. That is genuinely accessible, and it is accessible because you are lending the developer your money until December 2031.

Two things follow. First, this cannot deliver residency for five years, because the permit needs registered ownership. Second, ask what happens if the handover slips: what the contract says about delay, what you are owed, and what happens to your instalments. A five-year presale is a bet on the developer surviving five years — NEXT has the track record to make that reasonable, but it is still the question to ask, in writing.
