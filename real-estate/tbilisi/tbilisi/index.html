Tbilisi rents twelve months a year

This is the argument for the capital over the coast. Batumi's rental year has a shape — a loud summer and a quiet winter. Tbilisi does not: embassies, companies, students and long-stay visitors keep demand flat across the calendar. Lower peak, higher floor.

Avlabari is the old quarter on the high bank, walking distance to the Old Town and the sulphur baths. Daikin VRF air conditioning, Geberit plumbing rated to save 30% of water, low-emissivity glass, fibre-reinforced concrete. A gym, 24/7 concierge, valet parking, laundry, a business lounge, a library, children's areas and a garden lobby. Deposits start at $1,000–$3,000 with 0% instalments of 12–45 months.
