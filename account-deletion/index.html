Delete your Patroni Georgia account

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the app or from this website. Nothing has to go through us.

In the app

Open Patroni, tap your name at the top of the screen, scroll to Delete my account, type your password and confirm.

On this website

Go to your account page, sign in, and use Delete my account at the bottom.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address and telephone number.
  • The stored form of your password. We never hold the password itself.
  • Every listing you posted, and every photograph and video that came with it.
  • Your signed-in devices.

It happens immediately and it cannot be undone. Nothing is kept for later, and there is no copy to restore from.

Message us on WhatsApp at +90 541 654 2996 from the number on your account, or write to us, and we will delete it for you.
